# Purpose

Ensure every significant design decision is properly evaluated,
documented and communicated.

# Before Making the Decision

- [ ] Problem is clearly defined.
- [ ] Constraints are identified (time, budget, technology, team).
- [ ] Relevant stakeholders have been consulted.
- [ ] Existing architecture and decisions have been reviewed.
- [ ] At least two alternatives have been evaluated.
- [ ] Quality attribute impact has been assessed.
- [ ] Risk of the decision is understood.

# Decision Documentation

- [ ] ADR is drafted with context, decision and consequences.
- [ ] Alternatives are documented with reasons for rejection.
- [ ] Trade-offs are explicitly stated.
- [ ] Positive and negative consequences are listed.
- [ ] Decision is linked to related ADRs and documents.
- [ ] Decision is stored in the ADR directory.

# After the Decision

- [ ] Decision is communicated to affected teams.
- [ ] Decision log is updated.
- [ ] Implementation tasks are created if needed.
- [ ] Follow-up review is scheduled (if appropriate).
