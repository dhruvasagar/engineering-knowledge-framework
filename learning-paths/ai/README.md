# Purpose

These learning paths provide a structured progression through AI
engineering, from using AI as an individual contributor to establishing
AI practices across an organization.

# Beginner Path

## Objective

Use AI effectively for individual engineering tasks with proper
verification.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with basic engineering workflows.

## Topics

1. ***AI Engineering Principles***
   - AI augments, does not replace.
   - Human responsibility is final.
   - Reference: [AI Engineering Handbook](../../handbooks/ai/README.md)

2. ***Prompt Engineering Basics***
   - Be specific, provide context, iterate.
   - Reference: [Prompt Engineering Guide](../../guides/ai/prompt-engineering.md)

3. ***Using AI for Code Review***
   - AI as a first pass; human as the decision-maker.
   - Practice: Use AI to review a pull request and evaluate its
     suggestions.
   - Reference: [AI Workflows for Engineering](../../prompts/engineering-ai-workflows.md)

4. ***Verification Basics***
   - Never trust, always verify.
   - Reference: [AI Safety Guide](../../guides/ai/ai-safety.md)

## Assessment

Demonstrate by: Using AI to assist with a coding task and correctly
identifying which suggestions to accept and which to reject.

# Intermediate Path

## Objective

Design effective AI workflows for team processes and evaluate AI output
critically.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Beginner Path or equivalent experience.

## Topics

1. ***Context Engineering***
   - Providing structured context for better AI output.
   - Reference: [Context Engineering Guide](../../guides/ai/context-engineering.md)

2. ***AI Workflow Patterns***
   - Draft and refine, constrain and generate, analyse and advise.
   - Reference: [AI Engineering Handbook](../../handbooks/ai/README.md)

3. ***AI for Architecture***
   - Using AI for trade-off analysis and design exploration.
   - Reference: [AI Workflows for Architecture](../../prompts/architecture-ai-workflows.md)

4. ***AI Safety Practices***
   - High-risk use cases and prohibited uses.
   - Reference: [AI Safety Guide](../../guides/ai/ai-safety.md)

## Assessment

Demonstrate by: Designing an AI workflow for a team process.
Evaluating AI-generated architecture suggestions critically.

# Advanced Path

## Objective

Establish AI practices across teams, mentor others and define
organizational AI standards.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Intermediate Path or equivalent experience.

## Topics

1. ***AI Integration Strategy***
   - Integrating AI into team workflows.
   - Measuring AI impact on productivity and quality.

2. ***AI Risk Management***
   - Identifying and mitigating AI risks.
   - Compliance and governance for AI usage.

3. ***Mentoring AI Practices***
   - Training teams on effective AI use.
   - Reviewing AI usage patterns for improvement.

4. ***AI and Engineering Culture***
   - Balancing AI acceleration with engineering judgement.
   - Maintaining quality standards in an AI-assisted workflow.

## Assessment

Demonstrate by: Establishing AI practices for a team. Mentoring others
in effective AI use. Defining AI usage standards.
